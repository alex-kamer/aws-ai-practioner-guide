# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Offers quick-reference material for matching AWS AI/ML services to use cases and recalling core terminology.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Service matching cheat-sheet
The repo SHALL contain a cheat-sheet mapping each in-scope AWS AI/ML service to its purpose, a typical use case, and a "not to be confused with" note.

#### Scenario: Matching a use case to a service
- **WHEN** the learner has a use case such as "extract text from scanned forms"
- **THEN** the cheat-sheet identifies the matching service and distinguishes it from similar services

### Requirement: Glossary of core terms
The repo SHALL contain a glossary defining core AI/ML and generative AI terms in plain language, each in one to three sentences.

#### Scenario: Looking up a term
- **WHEN** the learner looks up a term such as "RAG" or "temperature"
- **THEN** the glossary gives a short definition and its relevance to the exam

### Requirement: Fits a pre-exam skim
The cheat-sheets SHALL be short enough to be reviewed in under one hour in total.

#### Scenario: Last-minute review
- **WHEN** the learner has one hour before an exam
- **THEN** they can read both cheat-sheets in full
